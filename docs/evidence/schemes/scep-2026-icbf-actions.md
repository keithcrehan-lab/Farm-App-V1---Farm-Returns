# SCEP 2026 — ICBF action evidence extract

Source URL: https://www.icbf.com/suckler-carbon-efficiency-programme-scep/
Publisher: Irish Cattle Breeding Federation
Retrieved: 2026-11-05
Source tier: authoritative technical source used by the programme

This local evidence extract records the action rules Farm Return encodes for SCEP Scheme Year 4 (2026). It is not a copy of the full page.

Verified facts used by Farm Return:

- Scheme Year 4 requires at least 50% of the yearly reference number to be calved as eligible beef-breed animals.
- Action 1 Eligible Bull/Eligible AI increases to 85% in Scheme Years 3 and 4 and 90% in Scheme Year 5.
- Action 2 Female Replacement Strategy had a 65% checkpoint by 31 October 2025 and a 75% checkpoint by 31 October 2027; there is no new percentage checkpoint stated for 2026.
- Action 3 Genotyping requires samples equivalent to at least 70% of the yearly reference number each year, rounded down to the nearest whole animal.
- Genotype samples for Scheme Year 4 must be returned by 30 November 2026.
- Action 4 requires at least 80% of eligible calves born on the holding, up to the yearly reference number, to be weighed with their dams.
- Each live calf used for Action 4 must be unweaned, at least 50 days old, and weighed with its dam on the same day.
- Weights should be submitted within 7 days of weighing and no later than 1 November annually.
- Action 5 requires calving details and annual ICBF surveys.
- The survey material states that calves born in the herd must be maintained on the holding for at least 5 months for the survey requirement.

Interpretation boundaries used by Farm Return:

- Genotyping target = floor(0.70 × yearly reference number), because ICBF explicitly states rounding down.
- Weighing target = floor(0.80 × min(eligible calves born, yearly reference number)), consistent with ICBF's own FAQ example.
- Farm Return does not infer an Action 2 percentage checkpoint for 2026.
