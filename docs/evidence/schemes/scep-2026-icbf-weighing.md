# SCEP 2026 — ICBF weighing evidence extract

Source URL: https://www.icbf.com/scep-2026-weighing-requirements/
Publisher: Irish Cattle Breeding Federation
Retrieved: 2026-11-05
Source tier: authoritative technical source used by the programme

This local evidence extract records only the 2026 weighing facts Farm Return encodes. It is not a copy of the full source page.

Verified facts used by Farm Return:

- Scheme Year 4 (2026) requires at least 80% of eligible calves born on the holding, up to the yearly reference number, to be weighed with their dams.
- The 2026 weighing submission deadline is 5:30pm on 1 November 2026.
- A live calf used for the action must be at least 50 days old and unweaned.
- The calf and its dam are weighed on the same day.
- The resulting weights must be submitted to ICBF.

Farm Return uses this source for the 2026 Action 4 target and its displayed deadline. It does not infer a payment penalty from this page.
