This local evidence extract records current Scheme Year 4 dates and examples. It is not a copy of the source pages.

Verified facts used by Farm Return:

- Scheme Year 4 calving period is 1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026.
- A yearly reference number of 30 therefore requires at least 15 eligible calves born in that period.
- A yearly reference number of 30 requires 21 genotypes for the 70% annual genotyping action.
- The 2026 genotype submission deadline is 30 November 2026.
- The 2026 weighing deadline is 5:30pm on 1 November 2026.
- Action 4 requires at least 80% of eligible calves born, up to the yearly reference number.
- For 2026, the yearly-reference amendment window ran from 22 January through 23:59 on 12 February 2026.
- If no amendment was submitted, the 2025 yearly reference number rolled forward automatically to 2026.
- A 2026 yearly reference number cannot exceed the programme reference number.
- The 2026 yearly reference number cannot be less than 80% of the previous year's yearly reference number.

Farm Return uses the amendment-window facts only as historical/contextual information in the 2026 card. It does not present the closed amendment window as a current action the farmer can still perform.
