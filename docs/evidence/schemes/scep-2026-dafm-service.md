# SCEP 2026 — DAFM service evidence extract

Publisher: Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine
Retrieved: 2026-11-05
Source tier: DAFM official

This local evidence extract records only the facts Farm Return encodes from the public DAFM service page. It is not a copy of the full page.

Verified facts used by Farm Return:

- SCEP is a five-year CAP programme supporting beef farmers to improve the environmental sustainability of the national beef herd.
- Ongoing qualifying conditions include an active herd number with Herd Owner Status, an annual BISS application on which all land parcels are declared, continued SBLAS membership, and calving at least 50% beef-breed animals of the yearly reference number in every scheme year.
- Programme payment is area-based.
- The programme reference number determines a maximum payable area by dividing the reference number by 1.5.
- Subject to compliance, payment is €225 per hectare for the first 15 hectares and €180 per hectare thereafter up to the maximum payable area.
- To receive the maximum payment, determined eligible forage hectares under the annual BISS application must at least match the maximum payable area.
- If determined eligible forage area is below the maximum payable area but is at least 80% of it, payment is based on that eligible forage area.
- The programme has five main actions: Eligible Bull/Eligible AI; Female Replacement Strategy; Genotyping; Weighing and Submission of Weights to ICBF; Calving Details and Surveys.
- New-entry applications originally opened 20 March 2023 and closed 22 May 2023, with the late-application rules applying to the first programme year only.
