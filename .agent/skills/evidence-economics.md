# Skill — Evidence & Economics

Load this skill only when a Plan phase touches scientific recommendations, regulatory constraints, economic optimisation, market assumptions, material-change logic or procurement.

## Evidence discipline

- Never invent production science, regulation, prices, thresholds, yields, conversion factors or financial assumptions.
- Preserve source identity, publication/version where available, effective date, applicability and uncertainty.
- Keep observed/actual, calculated, estimated, assumed, indicative and unknown states distinct.
- Unknown is never zero.
- A recommendation requiring unavailable evidence must fail honestly rather than fabricating precision.

## Economic discipline

Operational decisions should compare incremental expected net margin, subject to biological, legal, operational and risk constraints.

Do not optimise for gross sale value alone.
Do not treat existing farm-produced stock as free.
Distinguish:
- historical production cost,
- current stock/asset value,
- forward decision/replacement/opportunity cost.

Do not add farmer labour opportunity cost or detailed owned-machinery depreciation in V1 unless an explicitly authorised phase introduces an evidence-backed model.

## Strategy generation

Where evidence permits, present a small set of credible strategies rather than forcing the farmer to calculate targets. Strategy labels may express a meaningful trade-off such as lower input, recommended risk-adjusted expected return and higher output, but only when the underlying model genuinely differentiates them.

Every strategy comparison must expose enough inputs and assumptions to reproduce the result.

## Material change

Monitor/recompute important assumptions, but alert on decision impact rather than raw market noise.
No notification threshold is authoritative merely because it appears in a UI concept or campaign discussion. Implement thresholds only with explicit phase authority and document whether they are evidence-backed, policy choices or provisional pilot settings.

## Procurement independence

Recommendation and strategy engines are independent from supplier commission or commercial relationships.
Procurement begins only after a genuine need survives strategy re-evaluation.

Compare quotes on total relevant economic impact, not headline €/tonne alone where quality/specification, delivery, quantity or expected outcome differ.

When a farmer accepts a real quote:
- preserve quote provenance and timestamp,
- replace the corresponding indicative assumption for that plan,
- recalculate affected economics,
- notify/review only when the impact is material under the implemented policy.

## Auditability

For important calculations retain or reconstruct from immutable/versioned evidence:
- canonical inputs,
- units,
- source/provenance,
- assumption status,
- calculation/rule version,
- intermediate decision-relevant calculations where needed,
- chosen strategy/override,
- resulting recommendation or state transition.
